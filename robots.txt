# robots.txt for trailtemps.com
#
# Search engines, and AI assistants looking something up for a person, are
# welcome. Collecting this site to train AI models is not permitted.
#
# The names in the first group are the ones their operators say gather
# training data. Each operator runs separate crawlers for search and for
# fetching a page a person asked about (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User,
# Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, Googlebot, Applebot). Those are deliberately
# NOT listed, so they fall under the allow-all group at the bottom.
#
# Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are control tokens, not crawlers: they
# tell Google and Apple not to train on pages their ordinary crawlers fetched.
# Blocking Google-Extended also stops the Gemini app citing this site. Google
# Search is unaffected.
#
# Names checked 2026-11-09. They change, and a misspelled or retired name
# fails silently, so recheck this list about once a year.

User-agent: Google-Extended
User-agent: Applebot-Extended

User-agent: Amazonbot

# Everyone else. The Content-Signal line (Cloudflare Content Signals Policy)
# states the same preference for any crawler not named above: index for
# search, use to answer a question, do not train.
